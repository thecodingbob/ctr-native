# Adventure Difficulty

Enable **Expanded Options** to choose a difficulty level when creating an Adventure.
Easy, Medium and Hard are available; enabling **Extra Difficulty Levels** also
adds Super Hard and Ultra Hard. With Expanded Options disabled, new Adventures
use Medium.

Difficulty is saved per Adventure slot and remains in effect if you later change
the global options. Existing saves use Medium. The save/load preview displays the
difficulty for slots that use a different setting.

## Effects

- **Easy** makes opponents more forgiving, limits how much their difficulty can
  increase and gives more assistance after losses.
- **Medium** keeps the original adaptive difficulty.
- **Hard, Super Hard and Ultra Hard** use progressively higher fixed opponent
  difficulties. Bosses receive an additional difficulty bonus, and losses do
  not make their weapons or item restrictions more forgiving.

Relic target times and Crystal Challenge time limits also change:

| Difficulty | Time compared with Medium |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Easy       | +20%                      |
| Medium     | Unchanged                 |
| Hard       | -10%                      |
| Super Hard | -20%                      |
| Ultra Hard | -30%                      |

Existing co-op time adjustments still apply.
